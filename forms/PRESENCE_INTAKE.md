# Presence Stack intake

Keep the completed version private. Do not commit personal answers. If an agent conducts the interview, it should ask one question at a time, explain unfamiliar terms, and summarize the answer for confirmation before moving on.

## 1. The person

- What name should the presence use?
- What roles does the owner currently hold?
- Who are the main audiences: self, family, team, community, clients, public?
- What does the owner never want the presence to claim or imply?
- What would make the presence feel recognizably "like me" without becoming a parody?

## 2. Desired outcome

- What should this system make easier in the first 30 days?
- Rank: private assistant, team/project work, public receptionist, research, writing/content, file organization, media library, home automation, other.
- What does "working" look like?
- What would count as annoying, invasive, or fake?

## 3. Channel

- Where does the owner already spend the most communication time: Telegram, Discord, Slack, iMessage, CLI/Desktop, elsewhere?
- Who will communicate with the presence?
- Is one conversation enough, or are channels/threads/projects needed?
- Are voice notes, images, or files important?
- What channel might be useful six months from now?
- Which channel should remain the private recovery/admin route?

See `docs/CHANNEL_CHOOSER.md`.

## 4. Source material and voice

- Which owner-created videos, podcasts, interviews, writing, documents, and examples may be studied?
- Which sources are public, private, sensitive, outdated, or off-limits?
- May the presence quote, summarize, or only learn patterns from each source?
- Is generated voice or likeness allowed? Where must it be disclosed?
- Which old beliefs or roles no longer represent the owner?
- Who can approve corrections to the Presence Profile?

## 5. Decision rights

For each category, choose: prepare only / act under standing authority / always request sign-off / forbidden.

- messages and email;
- scheduling;
- public posts and content;
- purchases and money;
- file creation, editing, moving, and deletion;
- account/security changes;
- client/customer commitments;
- family/personal communication;
- infrastructure changes;
- research and recommendations.

## 6. Presence loops

- Which recurring work should continue while the owner is occupied elsewhere?
- What should trigger it?
- What evidence must return afterward?
- How long may it wait for owner sign-off?
- What should happen when the owner does not answer?
- What should never be retried automatically?
