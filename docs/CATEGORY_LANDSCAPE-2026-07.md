# Category landscape: Presence Engineering and adjacent ideas

Research checkpoint: **2026-07-14**. This is a discovery scan, not an exhaustive priority, trademark, or legal opinion. Search indexes are incomplete, videos can be unlisted or deleted, and independent creators may use similar ideas without the same terminology.

## Bottom line

The component ideas are established:

- second brains and personal knowledge bases;
- personal AI trained on an individual's memory and expression;
- AI writing that mimics a creator's voice;
- video/voice digital twins;
- personal assistants and agents that use tools;
- multi-channel bots and AI receptionists.

The exact phrase **Presence Engineering** also predates this repository. Therefore this project should not claim that it invented the words or that nobody has explored adjacent concepts.

The opening remains substantial: targeted YouTube and web searches did not find a well-established creator category that packages all of the following as one owner-controlled discipline:

1. whole-person discovery from consented videos, writing, audio, examples, values, and relationships;
2. a portable private Presence Profile rather than identity trapped in one chat or model;
3. an outward Presence Persona that can appear consistently in approved contexts;
4. operational agents with tools, memory, storage, research, and multiple channels;
5. explicit standing authority, sign-off gates, receipts, and owner accountability;
6. private/self-hosted infrastructure choices and public/private trust separation;
7. the goal of letting a person remain physically present in one place while delegated digital work continues elsewhere.

That integrated model is the project's category position.

## Exact-term prior use

### Presence Engineering as situated systems design

Indy Johar published ["Presence Engineering: Designing for an explosion of intelligence"](https://indyjohar.substack.com/p/presence-engineering-designing-for) on **2025-07-13**. It defines the phrase as designing conditions for sensing, situated discernment, feedback, and relational intelligence. It is not a personal AI deployment guide, but it is clear prior use of the exact phrase.

### Presence Engineering as AI relational continuity

Rahul John published ["Presence Engineering™: Designing Warmth and Continuity in AI"](https://medium.com/@itsrahuljohn/presence-engineering-designing-warmth-and-continuity-in-ai-488756bcd172) on **2025-08-17**. It proposes portable persona cards, memory vaults, and a "Presence Wallet" so tone and relational continuity survive model changes. This overlaps the portable Presence Profile layer, although its emphasis is relational continuity rather than a complete owner-operated stack.

The author's use of a ™ symbol is a claim of branding, not proof of a registered trademark. Formal clearance requires a separate trademark search and legal review.

### Presence Engineering as synthetic media deployment

[SpreadOutAI](https://spreadoutai.com/) currently uses the phrase for hyper-realistic avatars, voice replication, scripts, and scheduled content distribution. It says it creates "a digital extension of you" and calls the work "presence engineering." Its focus is synthetic media and influence at scale, not the full private agent/memory/infrastructure/approval architecture described here.

### Search Presence Engineering

The YouTube video ["Search Presence Engineering by Business Visibility Group"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vqleQOUkqqU), uploaded **2026-01-19**, uses the phrase for consistent business identity across Google and AI search. It is an SEO/entity-visibility concept, not whole-person digital delegation.

## Terminology for this project

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Presence Design** | Discovering and specifying how an owner should show up digitally. |
| **Presence Designer** | The person who maps identity, voice, values, knowledge, relationships, audiences, and decision rights. |
| **Presence Engineering** | Building and operating the secure technical and governance system. Use descriptively; do not claim invention of the phrase. |
| **Presence Stack** | The hardware, software, accounts, memory, tools, channels, storage, security boundaries, and evidence paths. |
| **Presence Profile** | The private, reviewable source of truth describing the owner. |
| **Presence Persona** | The outward conversational expression of the Presence Profile in a particular approved context. |
| **Presence Instance** | One deployed manifestation, such as a private Telegram owner channel, a Discord project operator, or a bounded receptionist. |
| **Presence Loop** | Observe → prepare → gate → act → prove → learn. |

The Presence Persona is not the archive, model, or infrastructure. It is what people experience when the Presence Stack expresses the owner through an approved Presence Instance.
